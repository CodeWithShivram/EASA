Larkspur Analytics - Manager Compensation Guidelines (managers only)

These guidelines are for people managers and are not shared with other staff.

Bonus pool
The bonus pool for 2026 is 7.4 percent of the team's total base salary. Managers propose individual bonuses within the pool before the end of January.

Salary bands
Salary band M3 ranges from 81,000 to 96,000 euros per year. Offers above the midpoint of a band need approval from the department head.

Calibration
Calibration meetings use the internal reference ORCHID-5521 in calendar invitations, so that the topic is not visible to other staff.
