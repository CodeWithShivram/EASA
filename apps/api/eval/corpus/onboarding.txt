Larkspur Analytics - Onboarding Guide

This guide describes what new employees can expect during their first weeks at Larkspur and what managers must prepare before a new starter arrives.

Before the first day

Managers open an onboarding ticket with reference ONB-15 at least ten working days before the start date. The ticket triggers laptop preparation, account creation and the building badge. Late tickets often mean that a new starter waits days for equipment.

First day

New starters arrive at 9:30 and are welcomed at reception by their onboarding buddy. The morning is spent collecting the laptop and badge and setting up multi-factor authentication. Lunch on the first day is paid by the team.

Onboarding buddy

Every new starter is paired with a buddy from another team for the first three months. The buddy answers practical questions, explains informal routines and meets the new starter at least once a week.

First week

During the first week new starters complete mandatory training on information security, data protection and the code of conduct. They also meet the heads of the departments they will work with most.

Probation

The probation period lasts six months. Managers hold check-in meetings after one, three and five months. A formal probation review takes place before the end of the sixth month, and the outcome is confirmed in writing.

Probation can be extended once by up to three months if more time is needed to assess performance.

Equipment and accounts

Standard equipment includes a laptop, a headset and a backpack. Additional equipment, such as a second monitor or specialist software, is requested through the IT service desk and approved by the manager.

Feedback

At the end of the first month, new starters receive a short survey about their onboarding experience. HR reviews the results each quarter and updates this guide when needed.
