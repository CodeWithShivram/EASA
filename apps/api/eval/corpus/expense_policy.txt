Larkspur Analytics - Expense Policy

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable costs incurred while doing their jobs. This policy describes what can be claimed, how to claim and the deadlines that apply.

Submitting claims

Expenses are claimed in the finance system with an itemised receipt for every cost above 10 euros. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred. Claims older than 90 days are rejected without exception.

Credit card statements are not accepted as a substitute for receipts. If a receipt is lost, the employee completes a missing receipt declaration, which may be used no more than twice per year.

Client entertainment

Meals with clients are recorded under expense code EXP-4471. The claim must list the names of all attendees and the business purpose. The limit is 75 euros per person, including drinks and service.

Internal team meals are only reimbursed when a director has approved them in advance. Alcohol is not reimbursed for internal events.

Mileage

Employees who use their private car for business journeys can claim 0.30 euros per kilometre. Commuting between home and the usual office does not count as a business journey. Parking fees and tolls can be claimed with receipts.

Corporate card

Employees who travel more than four times a year receive a corporate card. The card is for business expenses only. Personal purchases made by mistake must be repaid within ten days and reported to finance.

Corporate card statements must be reconciled in the finance system by the fifth working day of each month.

What cannot be claimed

The company does not reimburse traffic fines, personal entertainment such as films or minibar items, upgrades that were not approved, or the cost of lost personal belongings.

Approval and payment

Claims are approved by the line manager. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary if they are approved before the 20th of the month.

Repeated late or incomplete claims are reported to the department head.
