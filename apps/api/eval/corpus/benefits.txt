Larkspur Analytics - Employee Benefits

In addition to salary, Larkspur offers a range of benefits. This overview summarises them; the full terms are in the benefits handbook in the HR portal.

Health insurance

All employees are covered by the company health insurance plan from their first day. Family members can be added at a discount. Changes to the plan can only be made during the annual enrolment window in November, or within 30 days of a life event such as marriage or the birth of a child.

Pension

Larkspur matches employee pension contributions up to 5 percent of base salary. Employees are enrolled automatically and can choose to contribute more. The company contribution vests immediately.

Wellbeing

Employees receive a gym subsidy of up to 30 euros per month, paid with the salary on presentation of a membership invoice. A free and confidential employee assistance programme offers counselling sessions by phone or video.

Eye tests for screen users are paid by the company every two years. If glasses are needed only for screen work, the company contributes up to 150 euros.

Learning budget

Each employee has a personal learning budget of 1,000 euros per year for courses, conferences and books related to their role. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.

Bicycle scheme

Through the cycle-to-work scheme, employees can lease a bicycle and accessories. The monthly cost is deducted from gross salary, which reduces income tax.

Life assurance

Life assurance pays four times the annual base salary to the nominated beneficiaries if an employee dies while employed. Employees should keep their nomination form up to date in the HR portal.

Employee discounts

Employees get discounts with partner companies, including mobile phone contracts and cinema tickets. The current list is published on the intranet.
