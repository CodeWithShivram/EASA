Larkspur Analytics - Office and Facilities Guide

This guide covers practical matters in the Larkspur office, from opening hours to parking and safety.

Opening hours

The office is open from 7:00 to 21:00 on working days. Badges do not work outside these hours. Employees who need access at the weekend must request it from facilities at least two working days in advance.

Desk booking

Desks are shared and booked through the desk booking app up to two weeks ahead. Bookings that are not checked in by 10:30 are released automatically. Lockers are available on each floor for personal belongings.

Parking

The office has a small car park with 40 spaces. Parking permits are allocated by facilities with priority for employees with disabilities and car-sharing groups. Electric vehicle charging points are free for employees but limited to four hours per session.

Secure bicycle storage and showers are available in the basement.

Visitors

All visitors must be registered in the visitor system before they arrive. Visitors receive a temporary badge at reception and must be accompanied by their host at all times.

Fire safety

Fire drills are held twice a year. When the alarm sounds, leave the building by the nearest exit, do not use the lifts and gather at the assembly point in the park across the street. Fire wardens on each floor check that everyone has left.

Kitchens and food

Each floor has a kitchen with free coffee, tea and fruit. Food left in the fridges is cleared every Friday afternoon.

Lost and found

Lost property is kept at reception for one month. Items that are not collected are donated to charity.

Reporting problems

Broken equipment, heating problems or cleaning issues are reported through the facilities request form on the intranet. Urgent issues such as water leaks should be reported to reception by phone.
