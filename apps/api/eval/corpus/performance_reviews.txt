Larkspur Analytics - Performance Review Process

Performance reviews help employees understand how they are doing and plan their development. This document describes the yearly cycle and how ratings and promotions are decided.

Review cycle

Larkspur runs one formal review each year, in January. Employees write a self-assessment, collect feedback from at least three colleagues and then meet their manager for a review conversation. A shorter mid-year check-in takes place in July.

Goals

At the start of each year, employees agree three to five goals with their manager. Goals should be specific and measurable, and at least one of them should focus on personal development.

Ratings

Performance is rated on a four-point scale: needs improvement, solid, strong and exceptional. Ratings consider both results and the way they were achieved, including collaboration and adherence to company values.

Calibration

Before ratings are shared, managers in each department meet in calibration sessions to compare ratings and apply the scale consistently. HR facilitates the sessions. An individual manager cannot change a rating after calibration without HR approval.

Salary and bonus

Salary increases take effect on the first of April and depend on the rating and the position in the salary band. Bonuses are paid in March for employees who joined before the first of October of the previous year.

Promotions

Promotion decisions are made twice a year, in April and October. A manager nominates the employee with a written case, which a promotion panel reviews. Employees cannot nominate themselves, but they can ask their manager to consider a nomination.

Improvement plans

Employees rated needs improvement receive a performance improvement plan lasting 90 days. The plan lists clear expectations, the support offered and the dates of progress reviews. Most plans end with the employee meeting the expectations.

Disagreeing with a rating

An employee who disagrees with a rating can ask for a review by the manager's manager within 14 days of the review conversation.
