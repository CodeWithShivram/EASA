Larkspur Analytics - Remote Work Policy

Larkspur supports flexible working. This policy explains where and when employees may work outside the office and what the company provides to make that possible.

Eligibility

Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely for up to three days per week. Some roles, such as facilities and reception staff, require full-time presence on site and are excluded.

Core hours

Remote and office workers alike must be available between 10:00 and 15:00 local time on working days. Outside core hours, employees can arrange their schedule freely as long as their contracted hours are met.

Equipment

The company provides a laptop, a monitor, a keyboard and a headset for home use. Employees also receive a one-off home office allowance of 300 euros for a desk or chair, which can be claimed in the first six months of remote work.

A monthly internet allowance of 25 euros is paid with the salary to every employee who works remotely at least one day per week.

Working environment

Employees are responsible for a safe and quiet workspace. Confidential calls should not be taken in public places such as cafes or trains. Screens must be locked whenever the laptop is left unattended.

Working from another country

Working from another country is limited to 20 working days per calendar year and needs approval from HR in advance because of tax and immigration rules. Working from countries under international sanctions is not allowed.

Team days

Each team agrees on at least one shared office day per week, called the anchor day, when everyone is expected in the office for planning and collaboration.

Ending remote work

The company can ask an employee to return to the office if remote work is affecting performance or team collaboration. At least one month of notice is given.
