Larkspur Analytics - Leave Policy

This policy explains the paid and unpaid time off available to employees of Larkspur Analytics, a fictional company used for evaluation. It applies to all permanent staff. Contractors follow the terms of their own agreements.

Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata amount based on their contracted hours. New starters accrue leave from their first day.

Up to five unused days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days must be taken before the end of March, otherwise they expire. Managers cannot approve additional carry-over except where a medical absence prevented the employee from taking leave.

Requesting leave

Leave is requested in the HR portal using form LV-102 at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days. Shorter absences need only one working day of notice. Requests are approved by the line manager, who should respond within three working days.

During the year-end shutdown the office closes between Christmas Eve and New Year's Day. Employees must reserve three days of their annual allowance for the shutdown period.

Sick leave

Employees who are unwell should tell their manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. Up to seven calendar days may be self-certified. From the eighth day a doctor's note is required.

Larkspur pays full salary for the first 20 days of sickness absence in any rolling twelve-month period. After that, statutory sick pay applies.

Parental leave

Birth parents are entitled to 26 weeks of parental leave at full pay. Partners receive four weeks at full pay, which can be taken in one block or in weekly blocks within the first year after the birth or adoption.

Other leave

Employees may take up to five days of paid compassionate leave following the death of a close family member. Two days of paid volunteering leave are available each year for work with registered charities. Jury service is paid in full.

Unpaid leave of up to three months may be granted for personal reasons after one year of service, subject to business needs.
