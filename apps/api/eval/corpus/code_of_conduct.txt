Larkspur Analytics - Code of Conduct

The code of conduct describes the behaviour Larkspur expects from everyone who works for or on behalf of the company. It applies in the office, at client sites, at company events and online.

Respect at work

Harassment, bullying and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated. This includes comments, jokes or messages that a reasonable person would find offensive. Concerns can be raised with a manager, with HR or through the confidential reporting line.

Gifts and hospitality

Employees may accept gifts from suppliers or clients worth up to 50 euros. Gifts above that value must be declined or declared in the gifts register within five working days. Cash and vouchers must always be refused, whatever their value.

Hospitality such as a business lunch is acceptable when it is modest and has a clear business purpose. Invitations to travel or to stay in hotels at a supplier's expense need approval from the compliance officer.

Conflicts of interest

A conflict of interest exists when personal interests could influence work decisions, for example hiring a relative or holding shares in a supplier. Employees must declare potential conflicts to their manager and record them in the conflicts register.

Outside activities

Second jobs and paid board positions require written approval from HR, so that the company can check for conflicts and working time limits. Unpaid volunteering does not need approval.

Confidential information

Company and client information must not be shared outside the company unless there is a business need and an agreement in place. This obligation continues after employment ends.

Speaking up

Employees who suspect fraud, bribery or other serious misconduct can report it anonymously through the whistleblowing line run by an independent provider, Clearwater Ethics. Larkspur does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

Breaches

Breaches of this code are investigated by HR and may lead to disciplinary action, up to and including dismissal.
